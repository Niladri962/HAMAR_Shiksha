# HAMAR SHIKSHA — Teacher Guide

## Signing in
- Open `/teacher/login` (the green page). Your login ID is your **employee ID**.
- Your account works only after the education office has **verified** your identity and posting.
- Change the temporary password at your first sign-in (at least 10 characters).
- Forgotten password: use *Forgot password?* on the login page. The reset link goes to your
  registered mobile or e-mail.

You see only the classes and subjects approved for you. To change them, or to move to another
school, ask your block or district office.

## Tabs
**Today.** Your school, classes, today's timetable and teaching suggestions to review.

**This week's priorities.** Students whose answers show a learning outcome needing practice,
with the evidence (for example "2 of 7 marks on DEMO-MATH5.2 Fractions"). Use it to plan
small-group time.

**Teacher Saathi** (your AI assistant; it drafts, you decide):
- *Academic plan*: the year's learning outcomes spread over the school calendar. Press
  **✓ Mark taught** when you finish one; this works offline too. The bars show planned,
  taught and learned.
- *Lesson plans*: choose a learning outcome, then **Draft lesson plan**. The draft cites the
  textbook pages it used. Edit it, then **Approve**. Only approved plans can be used for
  substitute lessons.
- *Ask Saathi*: curriculum questions answered only from the approved textbooks, with book,
  chapter and page. If the books do not cover it, Saathi says so.
- *Worksheets*: draft multiple-choice questions from the textbook. They stay as drafts, invisible
  to students, until you approve each one into the question bank.
- *Remediation*: turn a class learning gap into a small-group plan. Approving it records the
  intervention in each student's learning record.
- *Micro-courses*: 15–20 minute courses; the ones marked "recommended" match subjects you teach
  without a recorded qualification.
- *Substitute lesson* (head teacher): when a teacher is absent, launch an approved lesson plan
  for that class. A supervisor must stay with the class; the AI does not replace a teacher.
- *🎬 AI video lesson*: when a subject teacher is not available (or for revision / catch-up), choose
  the class and subject, type the topic as it appears in the textbook and press **Make video lesson**.
  Gyan writes a short lesson from that class's textbook pages (1-2 minutes on the school server):
  scenes with pictures, narration in Hindi or English, captions and a 3-question quiz. Press ▶ to
  preview it, correct any scene (heading, spoken narration, on-screen points), then **Publish to
  class**. Students find it under *Learn → Video lessons* and on their home screen; the list shows
  how many have watched it. Head teachers can make lessons for any class in the school. If the
  model is not available, the lesson is built from the textbook passages and labelled so.

**Pariksha** (assessments):
- *Review queue*: short answers the engine could not mark with confidence, and all descriptive
  answers. Give marks; they count only after you confirm.
- *Question bank*: approved and draft questions by learning outcome and difficulty. Add your own.
- *Build assessment*: choose learning outcomes, question types and difficulty; the engine picks
  approved questions.
- *Competencies & misconceptions*: per learning outcome, how many students achieved it or need
  practice, and which wrong option was most common (with the likely misconception).

**Working offline.** The pill at the top shows ● Online or ○ Offline and how many changes are
waiting. Attendance and "Mark taught" can be saved without internet and are sent automatically
when the connection returns. Open the app once while online each day so the class lists are
up to date.

**Attendance.** Choose a class and date and mark P / A / L / Lv. *Mark all present* saves time.
You can correct attendance for up to 7 days.

**Assignments.** Create homework, worksheets or projects. Open *View* to read submissions and
write feedback.

**Assessments.**
- *Quiz / unit test / baseline:* write multiple-choice questions and select the correct option.
  Students take them on their phones and are graded automatically.
- *Offline:* for a paper test, set the maximum marks, then open *Results* and enter each
  student's marks.
- Publish when ready. Students never see the answer key before they submit.

**Class progress.**
- Topic mastery for each subject you teach, plus a grid of students by topics.
- *Analyse learning gaps* produces suggestions such as "revisit Fractions" and lists the
  students who may benefit from small-group practice.
- Click a student to see their progress. A class teacher can **reset a student's password**
  here; give the new password to the student or guardian in person.
- Download a class progress report (CSV / Excel / PDF). It contains student names, so keep it
  within the school.

**AI teaching assistant.**
- *Lesson plan:* enter the subject, class, topic, language and minutes to get a draft plan.
- *Quiz questions:* get draft questions. Press *Edit & save as assessment* to check and correct
  them before saving.
- The assistant **drafts** material; you decide what to use and you check it is accurate. No
  student information is sent to the AI service. If AI is not configured, you get a labelled
  offline template instead.

**Progress & follow-up.** Nothing to fill in — it is worked out from the milestones you mark and
your students' answers.
- *Progress against the academic plan*: each of your class-subjects gets a grade (**A** on track,
  **B** slightly behind, **C** behind plan, **D** needs support) with next steps: overdue outcomes
  to teach next, outcomes taught but not yet learned to re-teach. Head teachers can switch to the
  whole school.
- *Attendance follow-up*: children whose attendance is irregular, falling, or who have been absent
  for the last 4+ school days, with the lessons taught while they were mostly away (marked *catch
  up* if not yet learned) and what to do. It is a prompt for a caring call or home visit, never a
  label for the child.

**Digital resources.** Record a smart-class / ICT-lab / library / language-lab / tablet session in
one form (link it to a learning outcome if you can). Faster: when you **✓ Mark taught** in the
academic plan, pick the resource used in the same row. Report a fault with **Report fault**; head
teachers can register resources.

**Recognition.** Your badges (milestones on track, learning gains, micro-courses, digital
champion, regular attendance, doubts answered) — print a certificate for any badge you earn.
Appreciate students: the tab lists those who improved most recently; you can also choose any
student. Head teachers can appreciate colleagues. Messages are checked for phone numbers, links and
unkind words.

**Assistant review.** Questions students asked the learning helper that were flagged for
wellbeing or privacy. Follow up with care, according to your school's safeguarding procedure.

**Reports.** Submit your monthly academic report to the education office. Class figures are
attached automatically.

**Head teacher** (head teachers only).
- *Enrol a student (Workflow C):* enter the name and guardian details, and confirm that the
  guardian has given consent. You get a login ID and a temporary password; give them to the
  student or guardian in person.
- *Teacher attendance* for your school's staff.

## About suggestions
Suggestions are based only on your students' recorded answers. Each one shows its evidence (for
example "6 of 15 correct"). They are not labels or grades. Approve the ones you will act on and
dismiss the rest; nothing happens automatically.

# Student quick guide (हिंदी / English)

1. `/student/login` पर जाएँ (नारंगी पेज)। स्कूल से मिली **लॉगिन आईडी** और पासवर्ड डालें।
   Open `/student/login` (the orange page) and enter the login ID and password from your school.
2. पहली बार नया पासवर्ड बनाएँ। Make a new password the first time you sign in.
3. **होम / Home:** practice suggestions, homework and your badges.
4. **पढ़ें / Learn:** lessons and practice. Correct answers are shown in green.
5. **क्विज़ / Quizzes:** tests from your teacher. You can submit each one once.
6. **प्रगति / Progress:** your topics, results and attendance.
7. **पूछें / Ask:** ask the AI helper about your lessons. Answers marked
   **✓ पाठ्यपुस्तक से जाँचा गया / Checked against your textbook** show the book and page.
   If it says it could not verify, ask your teacher. Never share passwords or personal details.
   **Practice / अभ्यास** (in *Learn*, after choosing a subject) gives questions at your level.
   Without internet you can still take saved quizzes; your answers are sent later.
8. पासवर्ड भूल गए? अपने कक्षा शिक्षक से कहें। Forgot your password? Ask your class teacher.
