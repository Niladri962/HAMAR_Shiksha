# The 16 project objectives → what HAMAR SHIKSHA does

For evaluators and the department. Each row lists where to see the feature in the app, the API
and the test that proves it. "Limits" are stated honestly; full list in [LIMITATIONS.md](LIMITATIONS.md).
All figures in the demo are generated (`is_demo`); district and division names are real.

## Honest limits that matter for these objectives

- Grades and risk levels are **transparent rules**, not trained predictors. They have not been
  validated against real dropout or achievement data; thresholds need agreement with SCERT/SSA.
- The digital-infrastructure register is entered by schools; it is **not** connected to ICT@School,
  Samagra Shiksha or device telemetry. Use-versus-results is an association only.
- Badges reward activity and improvement; they are not used for appraisal and should not be.
- AI answers can be wrong even when cited (see LIMITATIONS.md "Verified does not mean correct").
