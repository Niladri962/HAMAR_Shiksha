## 2. Kruti Dev → Unicode conversion (Hindi-medium books)

SCERT's Hindi-medium PDFs are typeset in **Kruti Dev / DevLys 010**, a pre-Unicode font that draws
Devanagari on Latin code points. Their text layer reads `rkfydk&1 esa dqN tarqvksa` instead of
`तालिका-1 में कुछ जंतुओं`, which neither search nor the SLM can use. SCERT's own "Unicode" 2019-20
edition was tried and rejected: its text layer is also corrupted (e.g. `याभमसहॊ` for `रामसिंह`).

`app/kb/legacy_fonts.py` converts the text deterministically:

1. Words are rebuilt from the PDF's characters. Kruti Dev draws some vowel signs backwards over the
   previous glyph, so gaps are measured from the right-most edge seen in the word; otherwise words
   split as `हो ता`.
2. Only words set in a legacy font are converted, so English text in Times New Roman stays English.
3. Greedy longest-match mapping of Kruti Dev keys to Unicode (`vks` → ओ, `{k` → क्ष, `=` → त्र, …).
4. Reordering: `f` (ि) is typed before its consonant cluster and is moved after it; `Z` (reph र्)
   is typed after its syllable and is moved before it; an anusvara typed before a vowel sign is
   moved after it.
5. pdfplumber `dedupe_chars()` removes "fake bold" duplicates (headings drawn several times).
6. Ingestion refuses a converted document whose text is less than 60 % Devanagari.

Mark a document with `"legacy_font": "krutidev"` in a manifest, or choose *Kruti Dev* when
uploading in *Admin → Curriculum & AI*.

**Checked**: 7 word-level cases in `tests/test_voice_theme_kb.py`, and full pages read by eye from
each Hindi book. Example (Hindi-Chhattisgarhi Class 5, PDF p. 41):

> मोर ने सारे जंगल में ढिंढोरा पिटवा दिया कि सब पक्षी अपनी पसंद का रंग लगवाने के लिए मेरे पास आ जाएँ।

**Known defects**:
- In the Class 8 Hindi Science book, some Latin letters in English glosses are missing,
  e.g. `(Cheical Changes)` for *Chemical Changes*. The Hindi text is not affected.
- Equations, tables and diagrams come through as fragments or not at all, since text inside
  pictures is not read (there is no OCR).
- The rare ligatures that the mapping does not know are copied through unchanged.
- The Class 5 Hindi EVS book prints its chapter titles as images, so its passages cite the page
  but no chapter name.

### Answer quality on the real books (spot check, 15 Oct 2026, `llama3.2:3b` + bge-m3)

Retrieval found the right chapter and page for every question tried, but the **answers were often
wrong even when marked "Checked against your textbook"**:

| Question (student, Class 5) | Retrieved | Answer | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| *How can we prevent malaria?* (spoken, via voice input) | EVS Ch. 5 *Mosquitoes and Malaria*, p. 39 | Correct core ("prevent mosquitoes breeding") but turned the book's question *"is any insecticide sprinkled?"* into the advice *"Not sprinkling insecticides"* | Partly wrong |
| सोन के फर कहानी में सुखराम ने फर को कहाँ रखा? | Hindi-Chhattisgarhi पाठ-3, p. 24 | "अपने घर में" (the book: रँधनीघर के पठेरा, the kitchen shelf) | Vague |
| मलेरिया से बचने के लिए क्या करना चाहिए? | पर्यावरण अध्ययन, p. 40 | Includes invented advice ("रात में लाइट जलाएं…") | Wrong |
| छत्तीसगढ़ के पड़ोसी राज्य कौन-कौन से हैं? | पर्यावरण अध्ययन, p. 50 (the states are shown on a **map image**, not in the text) | Lists 3 states, one in the wrong direction; misses UP, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh | Wrong |

The grounding check verifies that the words come from the book, not that the meaning is right, and
it cannot see maps or pictures. These books therefore make retrieval real, but they do **not** make
the 3B model's answers reliable. Teacher-supervised use and SCERT-reviewed evaluation (docs/
LIMITATIONS.md) remain prerequisites; the benchmark in docs/MODEL_SELECTION.md was not re-run on
these books.

## 3. Passages and chapters

Textbook PDFs often have no blank lines between paragraphs, so passages are built line by line.
Each passage is about 120 words, never more than 200, breaks at a sentence end and never crosses a
page. Chapters are recognised from:
- `CHAPTER- 5` followed by a title line;
- `पाठ-4 …`;
- a chapter number above an upper-case title (`3` / `AIR`);
- for books with no such headings, the chapter name printed at the top of the pages (running
  headers).

## 4. Voice assistant

| | How | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|
| Speak a question | **Speak** records up to 30 s. The audio is sent to `/api/voice/transcribe` and the text is placed in the question box for the user to check and edit | Local **Whisper `small`** (MIT licence) on the school or state server. Audio is never stored |
| Hear the answer | **Listen** on each answer, or *Read answers aloud* | The device's own text-to-speech voices; on-device voices preferred |

Where it appears: AI Class → Ask (students) and Saathi → Ask (teachers). Configure with
`HS_STT_MODEL` (`small`, `base` or empty to disable). See docs/SECURITY_PRIVACY.md (Voice) and
docs/LIMITATIONS.md for privacy and accuracy limits.

Measured on the development laptop's CPU (Ryzen 7 7445HS), with English synthetic speech only:

| Model | 3 s question → text | Load time | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `base` | 1.1-2.7 s | 5 s (cached) | 145 MB |
| `small` | 3.2-4.4 s | first download about 2 min | 461 MB |

Both transcribed the three test questions correctly; `small` was used because Whisper's Hindi
accuracy improves considerably with model size. **Hindi accuracy has not been measured with real
speakers.**
