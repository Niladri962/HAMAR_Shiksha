## Chapter 1: Crop Production and Management

Crops grown in the rainy season are called kharif crops. They are sown in June-July. Paddy (rice), maize and soybean are kharif crops. Crops grown in winter are called rabi crops. They are sown in October-November. Wheat, gram and mustard are rabi crops.

The steps of crop production are: preparing the soil, sowing, adding manure and fertilisers, irrigation, protecting from weeds, harvesting and storage.

## Chapter 1: Crop Production and Management

Ploughing loosens the soil so that roots can breathe and go deep. Manure is made from decomposed plant and animal waste; it adds organic matter and improves the texture of the soil. Fertilisers are chemicals rich in particular nutrients such as nitrogen.

Unwanted plants that grow along with the crop are called weeds. Removing them is called weeding. Separating grain from the chaff after harvesting is called threshing.

## Chapter 2: Force and Pressure

A push or a pull on an object is called a force. Opening a door, kicking a ball and lifting a bag all involve force. A force can change the speed, the direction or the shape of an object.

Pressure is the force acting on a unit area: Pressure = Force / Area. The same force acting on a smaller area produces a larger pressure.

That is why a knife has a sharp edge, and why school bags and porters' loads have wide straps: the wider strap spreads the weight over a larger area and reduces the pressure on the shoulders.
