## Chapter 1: Water

We get water from rain, rivers, ponds, lakes, wells and hand pumps. Rain is the main source that fills rivers, ponds and the ground water that wells and hand pumps draw from.

Water from ponds and rivers may carry germs that cause diseases such as diarrhoea and typhoid. Boiling water for a few minutes kills most germs. Water can also be filtered through a clean cloth or a water filter, and it should be stored in a clean, covered container.

## Chapter 1: Water

Saving water: we can collect rainwater from rooftops and store it in tanks or lead it into a pit so that it soaks into the ground. This is called rooftop rainwater harvesting. It helps wells and hand pumps to keep water in summer.

Other ways to save water: close taps when not in use, repair leaking pipes, and reuse water from washing vegetables to water plants. A stone is not a source of water.

## Chapter 2: Plants and Seeds

A seed needs water, air and the right warmth to sprout. Sprouting is also called germination. Seeds do not need sunlight to germinate, but the young plant needs sunlight to make its food.

Seeds are spread in different ways. Light seeds with hair, like cotton, are carried by the wind. Coconuts are carried by water. Some seeds stick to the fur of animals, and birds spread seeds of fruits they eat.

Rice (paddy) is the main crop of Chhattisgarh. Because so much rice is grown here, Chhattisgarh is often called the rice bowl of India.
