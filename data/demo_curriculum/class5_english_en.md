## Unit 1: Nouns

A noun is a naming word. It names a person, a place, an animal or a thing: Meena, Bilaspur, cow, book.

A proper noun is the name of a particular person, place or thing, and it always begins with a capital letter: Mahanadi, Raipur, Sunita. A common noun is a general name: river, city, girl.

In the sentence "The cow eats grass", the nouns are cow and grass.

## Unit 2: Opposites

Opposites are words with opposite meanings. Examples: big - small, hot - cold, open - close, tall - short, happy - sad, day - night, early - late.

Practice: The opposite of hot is cold. The opposite of tall is short.
