## Chapter 1: Resources

Anything that can be used to meet a need is a resource. Renewable resources such as sunlight, wind and water can be replenished, and forests can regrow if they are managed carefully. Non-renewable resources such as coal and petroleum take millions of years to form and can be used up.

Chhattisgarh is rich in forests and in minerals such as coal, iron ore, bauxite and limestone. Using resources carefully so that they last for future generations is called conservation.

## Unit 3: Tenses

The tense of a verb tells us the time of an action. Present: I play. Past: I played. Future: I will play.

Time words help: yesterday points to the past ("Yesterday she went to school"), tomorrow points to the future ("Tomorrow we will play the match"), and every day shows a habit in the simple present ("He plays football every day").
